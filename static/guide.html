1. 即時評分、小組加分與紀錄回復 (Scoring & Logs) 1. Live Scoring, Group Scoring & Log Restore

⚡ 快速評分⚡ QUICK SCORING
快速加減分切換模式 (Quick Scoring Mode) Quick +/- Scoring Toggle Mode
在即時評分頁面上方，點擊 ⚡ 快速加減分：開啟/關閉 按鈕：
• 開啟狀態：學生卡片上直接常駐顯示 +1（綠色加分）與 -1（紅色扣分）按鈕，點擊1 秒直接完成評分。
• 🔒 自動鎖定上方小組標籤：開啟快速加減分時，系統會自動禁用（Disabled）上方小組篩選與加分按鈕並顯示灰階提示，防止誤觸導致全組連動加扣分；關閉快速加減分後即自動解鎖恢復小組操作。
• 手機自適應：在手機或平板直向螢幕上，加減按鈕具備最佳間距與防誤觸設計，操作流暢不重疊。
• 關閉狀態：恢復標準模式，點擊學生卡片跳出詳細評分規則選單（熱心助人、發言踴躍等）。 Click the ⚡ Quick Scoring: On/Off button on the top right of the scoring pane:
• Enabled: Permanent +1 (Green) and -1 (Red) buttons appear on student cards for rapid 1-tap scoring.
• 🔒 Auto-Disable Team Filter Buttons: While Quick Mode is active, team filter buttons at the top are automatically disabled with grayscale styling to prevent accidental bulk scoring. Disabling Quick Mode immediately unlocks team buttons.
• Mobile Optimized: Layout adapts perfectly to smartphone touch screens with anti-overlap spacing.
• Disabled: Standard popover menu mode opens when clicking student cards.
小組與多選Team & Batch
小組即時加分、多選批次評分與 2.5 秒撤銷 (Undo) Team Scoring, Batch Award & 2.5s Undo
• 小組即時加分：點選上方快捷小組標籤或整組成員進行評分時，系統會同步為全組學生加分，並為該小組累積「小組獨立得分」，卡片上即時呈現當前累積組分。
• 多選批次評分：長按或點選多位學生卡片進入多選狀態，點選評分項目可同時為多位同學加減分。
• 秒級撤銷 (Undo)：評分完成後，螢幕下方會跳出 2.5 秒 Undo 提示框，若誤觸可立即點擊「↩️ 復原撤銷」回到評分前狀態。 • Team Live Scoring: Awarding points to a team adds points to all members and accumulates Isolated Group Points simultaneously with live score badges on student cards.
• Batch Scoring: Select multiple students by tapping cards to award points in batches.
• Instant Undo: A 2.5s Undo toast appears at the bottom after scoring; tap "↩️ Undo" anytime to revert accidental scores.
日誌管理Log Manager
評分歷程分組標籤、記錄刪除與一鍵回復 (Restore) Score Logs with Group Tags & 1-Click Restore
• 分組模式與組別標籤：在學生個人評分歷程中，每筆加扣分皆完整註記評分時所屬的「分組模式」與「小組名稱」（例如：國語分組 · 第 2 組）。
• 誤刪一鍵回復：若教師在日誌中不小心刪除分數，可隨時點擊 ↩️ 恢復紀錄，系統會立即回補分數，確保學期歷程數據精準無虞。 • Group & Plan Tags: Each score entry in student history logs tracks its exact Grouping Plan and Team Name (e.g., Chinese Reading · Team 2).
• 1-Click Log Restore: If a score record is accidentally deleted, click ↩️ Restore Record to instantly restore the score.

即時評分時段統計切換（當天／當週／當月／指定期間） Live Scoring Period Filter (Today / Week / Month / Custom Range)
即時評分頁面標題旁新增 當天、當週、當月、指定期間 4 顆時段切換按鈕，學生卡片與小組上顯示的即時累積分數會立即依所選時段重新計算（例如切到「當週」只看本週加扣分總和），旁邊並會顯示 📅 分數統計：當天 之類的當前時段徽章。
點擊 指定期間 會展開專屬日期區間工具列，可自行輸入起訖日期，或點擊 近 7 天、近 30 天、本月 快捷標籤後按 ⚡ 套用範圍 立即套用。
教師的時段選擇會記住（存於瀏覽器），切換分頁、切換班級或重新整理頁面後仍維持上次選擇的統計時段。 The Live Scoring header now has 4 period toggle buttons: Today, This Week, This Month, Custom Range. Live cumulative scores shown on student and team cards instantly recalculate to match the selected period (e.g., switching to "This Week" shows only this week's point changes), with a badge like 📅 Score Period: Today next to it.
Clicking Custom Range expands a dedicated date-range toolbar — enter start/end dates manually, or click quick presets Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, or This Month, then press ⚡ Apply Range.
Your period choice is remembered in the browser, so it persists across tab switches, course changes, and page reloads.

🚶 早自修／午休在位掌握（學生不在教室的原因與負責老師） 🚶 Morning Study / Lunch Break Whereabouts (Why a Student Is Out & Who to Ask)
位於 📋 班級經營 ➔ 🚶 在位掌握，用來掌握早自修、午休時學生是否在教室；校隊訓練、處室公差時，導師可立刻知道人在哪裡、該問哪位老師。
• 使用方式：選擇日期與時段（🌅 早自修／🍱 午休，系統依目前時間自動選擇），預設全班都在教室；點學生卡片，選擇不在教室的原因即可；回來時再點一次，按「✅ 回到教室了」。系統會記下離開與回來的時間，紀錄不會消失；同一時段可以外出多次。誤按時可在視窗中按「🗑️ 誤按，刪除這筆」（刪除後不留紀錄）。
• 預設原因：棒球隊、羽球隊、合唱團、田徑隊、英文訓練、科展、奧匹、語文訓練、糾察隊、其他。選「其他」時必須輸入原因（例如「幫老師搬資料」）。
• 「不在教室名單」找人清單：依原因分組，直接顯示學生與負責老師，導師臨時找學生時一眼就知道該問誰。
• 🪑 座位表檢視：按「👥 名冊／🪑 座位表」切換，座位表依「⚙️ 設定 ➔ 座位表編排」的排位顯示（含講台位置），一眼看出哪個座位的同學不在教室；學生自行登記頁也有相同切換。
• ☑️ 多選：同一批學生（例如 3 位棒球隊員）可一次選取、一次設定原因。✅ 全班都在教室 可一鍵把該時段所有尚未回來的學生標記為已回教室（紀錄保留）。
• 📊 紀錄與統計：選擇日期區間與時段，查看學生統計（誰外出幾次、累計幾分鐘、各原因次數）、原因統計（各原因次數、人數、負責老師）與明細（離開–回來時間、外出分鐘）；「未登記回來」代表過了當天仍沒人標記回到教室，不計入累計分鐘。按「📥 匯出 Excel」可下載含「外出明細／學生統計／原因統計」三張工作表的報表。
• ⚙️ 管理原因與負責老師：可修改名稱與圖示、新增項目、停用或刪除，並為每個項目填寫負責老師（選填）。修改後自動儲存，所有班級共用；已有登記紀錄的項目不能刪除，只會改為停用。
• 🔗 學生自行登記頁（免登入）：教師不在教室時，可按「🔗 學生自行登記頁」啟用，系統會產生本班專屬的連結。教師電腦：按「開啟登記頁」後按 Ctrl + D 加入瀏覽器書籤，之後教師離開教室、系統未登入時，學生直接點書籤即可登記；手機／平板：掃描 QR Code。學生用任一方式開啟後，不需登入即可點自己的名字、選擇公出原因，回來時再點一次按「我回到教室了」（只會記下回來時間，不會刪除紀錄）；頁面同時顯示不在教室名單與負責老師。連結帶有本班專屬金鑰，只提供給本班學生；外流或不再開放時按「重新產生連結」或「停用」，舊連結立即失效。學生只能登記今天的狀態，看不到學生照片。同一批同學（例如一起去校隊練習）可按「☑️ 多人一起登記」，點選多位同學後一次選好原因。
• 多台裝置同時開啟時，教師端會即時同步，學生自行登記頁約每 10 秒自動更新。 Found at 📋 Class Management ➔ 🚶 Whereabouts: track whether students are in the classroom during morning study and lunch break, so a homeroom teacher instantly knows where a student is and whom to ask when they are away for team practice or office errands.
• How to use: pick the date and slot (🌅 Morning Study / 🍱 Lunch Break — auto-selected by the current time). Everyone defaults to "in class." Click a student card and choose the reason they are away; when they return, click again and press "✅ Back in class." The system records both the leave and return times and keeps the record; a student can leave several times in one slot. If tapped by mistake, use "🗑️ Wrong tap, delete this entry" (leaves no record).
• Default reasons: Baseball, Badminton, Choir, Track & Field, English Training, Science Fair, Olympiad, Language Training, Patrol Squad, Other. Choosing "Other" requires a typed reason (e.g., "helping a teacher move materials").
• "Not in Classroom" lookup list: grouped by reason and showing the students together with the responsible teacher, so you know whom to ask at a glance.
• 🪑 Seating View: toggle "👥 Roster / 🪑 Seating" to see students laid out as in "⚙️ Settings ➔ Seating Chart" (including the podium position), so you can see at a glance whose seat is empty; the student self-service page has the same toggle.
• ☑️ Multi-select: select a group (e.g., 3 baseball players) and set the reason once. ✅ Everyone in class marks every student still out in that slot as returned (records are kept).
• 📊 Records & Statistics: pick a date range and slot to see by student (how many times, total minutes, counts per reason), by reason (counts, headcount, responsible teacher), and details (leave–return times, minutes). "No return logged" means nobody marked the student back in class by the end of the day; it is excluded from total minutes. Click "📥 Export Excel" for a workbook with Details / By Student / By Reason sheets.
• ⚙️ Manage Reasons & Teachers: rename, change icons, add, deactivate or delete items, and fill in each item's responsible teacher (optional). Changes auto-save and are shared by all classes; items already used in records cannot be deleted, only deactivated.
• 🔗 Student Self-Service Page (no login): when you are away from the classroom, click "🔗 Student Self-Service Page" and enable it to get a class-specific link. On the teacher's computer: click "Open page," press Ctrl + D to bookmark it — afterwards, when you are away and the system is logged out, students just click the bookmark to register; on phones/tablets: scan the QR code. Either way, students can, without logging in, tap their own name, choose a reason for leaving, and tap again to press "I'm back in class"; the page also shows who is out and the responsible teacher. The link carries a class-specific key, so share it only with your students; use "Regenerate link" or "Disable" if it leaks or you no longer want it open — the old link stops working immediately. Students can only register today's status and cannot see student photos. A group leaving together (e.g., for team practice) can use "☑️ Register Multiple Students" to pick several names and choose the reason once.
• When open on several devices, the teacher view syncs in real time and the student page auto-refreshes about every 10 seconds.

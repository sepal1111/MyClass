登入密碼怎麼用Your Login Password

老師登入不需要帳號，只要輸入「每天會變的密碼」。第一次使用時，系統會請你設定自己的專屬字頭。Teachers need no account, just a password that changes every day. On first use the system asks you to set your own prefix.

密碼公式FORMULA
密碼 = 你的字頭 + 今天的月日Password = your prefix + today’s month and day
例如你的字頭是 Teacher，今天是 10 月 14 日，今天的密碼就是 Teacher1014。
同一台電腦登入後，當天都不用再輸入；隔天會再要求輸入一次。
請把字頭記起來，也不要告訴學生。知道字頭的人，就能算出每天的密碼。
If your prefix is Teacher and today is October 14, today’s password is Teacher1014.
After logging in on a computer you stay logged in for the day, and are asked again the next day.
Remember your prefix and do not tell students. Anyone who knows it can work out each day’s password.
第一次登入FIRST LOGIN
第一次登入：一定要先改字頭First login: you must change the prefix
1新安裝的系統，字頭預設是 Admin。第一次登入請輸入 Admin 加今天的月日，例如 Admin1014。
2登入後會跳出「請先修改密碼字頭」視窗，這個視窗無法關閉。
3輸入你想用的新字頭兩次（至少 4 個字，而且不能是 Admin），按 儲存並繼續。（如果畫面多了一格要你輸入「今日目前密碼」，就輸入 Admin 加今天月日。）
4完成。之後每天的密碼就是「新字頭 + 當天月日」。
沒改完字頭之前，系統其他功能都不能使用，這是為了避免大家都知道的預設密碼被拿來亂登入。
1A new installation uses Admin as the prefix. On first login type Admin plus today’s month and day, e.g. Admin1014.
2A window “Please change the password prefix” appears and cannot be closed.
3Type your new prefix twice (at least 4 characters, and not “Admin”) and click Save and continue. (If an extra box asks for today’s current password, type Admin plus today’s month and day.)
4Done. From now on your daily password is “new prefix + month and day”.
Nothing else works until the prefix is changed. This prevents the well-known default password from being abused.
忘記密碼FORGOT
忘記字頭怎麼辦？Forgot your prefix?
1在登入畫面按「忘記密碼」，再按「確定重置」。
2字頭會回到 Admin，用 Admin 加今天月日登入，再重新設定一個新字頭。
為了安全，這個重置只能在老師自己的電腦上操作；學生的手機、平板或其他電腦按了都沒有用。重置不需要舊字頭，所以請保管好這台電腦與隨身碟（見「常見問題」的「資料會不會外流」）。
1On the login screen click “Forgot password”, then confirm the reset.
2The prefix returns to Admin. Log in with Admin plus today’s month and day, then set a new prefix.
For safety this reset only works on the teacher’s own computer, not on students’ phones, tablets or other computers. It does not ask for the old prefix, so keep this computer and USB drive safe (see “Will my data leak?” in the FAQ).

讓學生與手機連線Connecting Students and Your Phone

學生用自己的手機或平板，掃描 QR Code 就能連到老師電腦上的系統。電腦和手機要連同一個 Wi-Fi。Students scan a QR Code with their own phone or tablet to reach the system on your computer. Computer and phone must be on the same Wi-Fi.

學生連線STUDENTS
讓學生登入Let students log in
1點 ⚙️ 設定，在右側按 📱 學生連線 QR Code。
2點一下 QR Code，它會放大到全螢幕，方便投影。
3學生用手機或平板的相機掃描，開啟登入頁，輸入帳號與密碼。
學生的帳號與密碼：匯入名單時可以自己填；沒填的話，帳號由系統自動產生，預設密碼是「學號」（沒有填學號的學生才用座號四碼，例如 5 號是 0005）。帳號不分大小寫（iPad 自動把第一個字大寫也能登入），密碼才分大小寫。學生第一次登入時，系統會要求他先自己設定新密碼（長度、內容都沒有限制，只要不是空白），設好才能使用；之後也能按右上角的 🔑 密碼 再修改。老師在「學生名冊管理」按 🔑 恢復預設密碼 後，學生下次登入又會被要求重新設定。名冊上每位學生都會標示 🔒 已自訂密碼 或 ⚠️ 尚未修改（預設密碼），方便你知道誰還沒換。
1Open ⚙️ Settings and click 📱 Student QR Code on the right.
2Click the QR Code to enlarge it to full screen for projecting.
3Students scan it with their phone or tablet camera, open the login page and enter their account and password.
Student accounts: you can fill them in when importing the roster. If left empty the system creates the account and the default password is the student ID (students without an ID use the 4-digit seat number, e.g. seat 5 = 0005). Accounts are not case-sensitive (an iPad capitalising the first letter still works); passwords are. On first login the system makes each student set a new password of their own (no rules at all, just not empty) before they can continue; later they can change it with the 🔑 button at the top right. When you press 🔑 Restore default password in the roster, the student is asked to set a new one again at next login. Each student in the roster is marked 🔒 Own password set or ⚠️ Not changed yet (default) so you can see who has not changed it.

2用手機掃描，輸入今天的老師密碼，選擇班級。
3就可以在手機上：點名、快速加減分、寫特殊表現紀錄、寫教學日誌，還能遙控電腦上的公佈欄、計時與抽籤。
手機上的加減分和點名會立刻同步到電腦與大螢幕。手機頁首也有「EN／中文」按鈕可以切換語言。

2Scan it with your phone, enter today’s teacher password and choose a class.
3On the phone you can take attendance, award points quickly, write notes and journal entries, and remote-control the bulletin, timer and draw on your computer.

密碼提醒HINT
💡 學生忘記密碼：先讓他看提醒，再由你恢復預設💡 A student forgot the password: show the hint first, then restore the default
• 學生可以在自己的頁面按 💡 提醒（或設定新密碼時一併填寫），把密碼分成三段寫下提醒：前綴、主要內容、後綴。主要內容必填、寫的是「那一段是什麼」，前綴與後綴至少填一個。
• 例如前綴 xy、主要內容「媽媽的電話號碼」、後綴 sd，提醒就會是「xy媽媽的電話號碼sd」。
• 學生在登入畫面輸入帳號後，密碼連續輸錯 3 次，畫面就會顯示這個帳號的提醒。
• 還是想不起來：到 ⚙️ 設定 ➔ 👨‍🎓 學生名冊管理，按該生那一列的 🔑 恢復預設密碼，密碼會變回學號；學生下次登入要重新設定密碼，原本的提醒也會清除。
提醒裡的前綴與後綴是密碼的真實內容，任何人輸入該帳號並連續輸錯都看得到，所以請提醒學生不要把整個密碼寫進去。名冊上有 💡 代表該生已寫下提醒（老師看不到內容）。
• Students can press 💡 Hint on their own page (or fill it in when setting the new password) and split the password into three parts: prefix, main content and suffix. The main content is required and describes what that part is; at least one of prefix and suffix is needed.
• For example prefix xy, main content “mom’s phone number” and suffix sd gives the hint “xy mom’s phone number sd”.
• After a student enters their account on the login page and gets the password wrong 3 times, the hint is shown.
• Still stuck: go to ⚙️ Settings ➔ 👨‍🎓 Student Roster and press 🔑 Restore default password on that row. The password returns to the student ID, the student must set a new one at next login, and the old hint is cleared.
The prefix and suffix are real parts of the password and anyone who types that account and fails repeatedly can see them, so remind students not to write the whole password. A 💡 in the roster means the student wrote a hint (teachers cannot see its content).
連不上TROUBLE
掃了 QR Code 卻打不開？QR Code scanned but page won’t open?
• 確認手機／平板和老師電腦連的是同一個 Wi-Fi。學校網路若有「訪客隔離」，可以改用手機分享熱點讓電腦連線。
• iPhone 連不上、平板卻可以：請先關閉 iPhone 的行動數據，只用 Wi-Fi 再試一次。
• 出現「此連線不是私人連線」是正常的（系統使用自己簽發的憑證）。點「顯示詳細資訊 ➔ 造訪此網站」就可以繼續。
• 網址要是 https:// 開頭。
• Make sure the phone/tablet is on the same Wi-Fi as your computer. If the school network isolates guests, share a hotspot from your phone and connect the computer to it.
• iPhone fails but tablet works: turn off the iPhone’s cellular data and use Wi-Fi only.
• A “This connection is not private” warning is normal (the system uses its own certificate). Tap “Show Details ➔ visit this website” to continue.
• The address must start with https://.
通知NOTIFY
🔔 學生會收到哪些通知？🔔 What notifications do students get?
• 學生頁面右上角的 🔔 鈴鐺 會出現紅點與未讀數，不用老師額外操作，也不用另外通知。
• 作業與測驗：已評分（含分數）、留下評語、被退回或解鎖可重新繳交、老師回覆了提問。
• 新內容：你新增教材、作業或測驗時（隱藏的、定時開放的不會通知；同一單元 10 分鐘內連續新增會合成一則）。
• 檔案蒐集：新建立並開放上傳，或由停止改回開放上傳時。
• 獎勵兌換：實體獎品申請被核准、領取完成、被駁回或取消。
• 學生點一則通知，就會直接跳到對應的分頁。每位學生最多保留最新 100 則。
日常加減分不會發通知，避免一堂課響個不停；學生可在「🌟 我的點數」看每一筆加分紀錄。
• A 🔔 bell at the top right of the student page shows a red dot and unread count; nothing extra for you to do.
• Homework and quizzes: graded (with score), comment left, returned or unlocked for resubmission, teacher replied to a question.
• New content: when you add a material, assignment or quiz (hidden or scheduled items are skipped; several added within 10 minutes in one unit merge into one).
• File collection: newly created and open, or switched from stopped back to open.
• Rewards: a physical prize request is approved, collected, rejected or cancelled.
• Tapping a notification jumps to the matching tab. Each student keeps the latest 100.
Everyday point awards do not send notifications, to avoid constant noise; students can see every award under “🌟 My Points”.

上課中最常用的功能Most-Used Features During Class

都在 🚀 課堂進行 底下。上課時，通常只需要這幾個。All under 🚀 In-Class. These few cover most lessons.

最常用MOST USED
⭐ 即時評分：幫學生或小組加分⭐ Live Scoring: award points
• 最基本的做法：點學生的卡片 ➔ 選評分項目（發言踴躍、認真聽講…）。
• 想一直連續加分：按上方 ⚡ 快速加減分，每張卡片會出現 +1 與 −1，點一下就加或扣 1 分。（開啟時，上方小組按鈕會暫時停用，避免誤觸。）
• 一次給好幾位同學：連續點選多張卡片，再選評分項目。
• 整組一起加分：點上方的小組標籤。小組也會累積自己的總分。
• 按錯了：加分後畫面下方會出現 2.5 秒的「↩️ 復原」，馬上按就取消。
• 事後想修改：到該生的評分歷程刪除那一筆；刪錯了可以按「↩️ 恢復紀錄」救回來。
• 想看這週或這個月的累計：標題旁的「當天／當週／當月／指定期間」按鈕可以切換，分數會依所選時段重新計算。
評分項目（例如「幫忙整理」+2 分）可以自己新增或修改，見「學期初設定」。
• Basic way: click a student card ➔ choose a scoring item (Active participation, Good listening…).
• Many points in a row: click ⚡ Quick +/− at the top. Every card shows +1 and −1; one tap adds or removes a point. (Group buttons are disabled meanwhile to prevent mistakes.)
• Several students at once: tick several cards, then choose an item.
• Whole group: click the group tag at the top. Groups also keep their own total.
• Made a mistake: a 2.5-second “↩️ Undo” appears at the bottom, click it right away.
• Fix it later: delete the entry in the student’s history; if deleted by mistake click “↩️ Restore”.
• Weekly or monthly totals: use the Today / Week / Month / Custom buttons beside the title.
You can add or edit scoring items (e.g. “Helping out” +2) under Settings.

計時抽籤TOOLS
🧰 計時、抽籤、公佈欄（教學互動工具）🧰 Timer, Draw, Bulletin (Teaching Tools)
• 隨時可用的小工具列：畫面右下角常駐一條小工具列，有 ⏱️ 快速計時、🎲 快速抽籤、🔔 專注鈴，在任何頁面都能直接按。

• 📌 Bulletin: write today’s homework or reminders, choose text size and colour, then ⛶ full screen to project it. It saves automatically.
• 🎲 Random draw: pick students or groups. Tick “Exclude already drawn” so everyone gets a turn.
• ⏱️ Timer: quick buttons from 30 seconds to 15 minutes, a countdown warning in the last 5 seconds and a bell at zero; also a stopwatch and background music, and a big full-screen countdown.

安裝、升級與資料備份Installing, Upgrading and Backups

有人替你安裝好的話，這一節可以略過。If someone installed it for you, you can skip this section.

兩種版本EDITIONS
可攜版與安裝版Portable and installed editions
• 可攜版（跑班的科任老師）：整個資料夾放在隨身碟，雙擊執行檔就能用，資料都在資料夾裡的 bin。
• 安裝版（固定在同一間教室的導師）：執行安裝程式，預設裝到 C:\MyClass。啟動後在背景執行，螢幕右下角通知區會出現圖示：雙擊開啟系統，右鍵可複製學生連線網址、開啟檔案資料夾、設定開機自動啟動或結束。
• 安裝時會自動允許通過 Windows 防火牆，學生的手機才連得進來。
• Portable (travelling subject teachers): keep the whole folder on a USB drive and double-click the program; all data lives in its bin folder.
• Installed (homeroom teachers in one room): run the installer, default C:\MyClass. It runs in the background and shows an icon at the bottom right: double-click to open; right-click to copy the student address, open the files folder, set start-up or quit.
• The installer allows the app through the Windows firewall so students’ phones can connect.
新版提示UPDATES
🔄 有新版本時會提醒你🔄 You are notified about new versions
• 系統會在背景定期向 GitHub 查詢是否有新版本。有的話，登入後畫面最上方會出現一條 「🆕 MyClass 有新版本」 的提示。
• 按 查看並更新，會看到更新內容與更新步驟；按 下載更新檔 就會下載對應你這個版本類型的檔案（可攜版是壓縮檔，安裝版是安裝程式）。系統不會自動更換檔案，一切由你決定。
• 可攜版的更新方式：下載新版並解壓縮（會得到一個新資料夾，裡面是 MyClass 執行檔和 system 資料夾，兩者要放在一起）➔ 關閉 MyClass ➔ 把舊資料夾裡的 bin 資料夾整個複製到新資料夾（與執行檔同一層）➔ 開啟新版。第一次啟動會先自動備份資料庫，確認班級資料都在後再刪除舊資料夾。安裝版則直接執行新的安裝程式即可，資料會保留。
• 不想現在更新：按 略過此版本，這個版本就不再提醒，出現更新的版本時才會再提醒；或按 ✕ 先關掉這次的提示。
• 要自己檢查或關閉：⚙️ 設定 ➔ 🔐 密碼與連線 的「🔄 軟體更新」卡片，可看到目前版本、按「立即檢查更新」，或取消勾選「自動檢查新版本」。
檢查時只會向 GitHub 詢問「最新版本號」，不會傳送任何班級、學生或個人資料；每 12 小時最多一次。學校網路若擋住外部連線，檢查會靜默失敗，不影響使用。
• In the background the system periodically asks GitHub whether a new version exists. If so, a “🆕 A new version of MyClass is available” bar appears at the top after you log in.
• Click View & update for the changes and the update steps, then Download update to get the file for your edition (a zip for portable, the installer for installed). The system never replaces files by itself.
• Portable edition: download and extract (you get a new folder with the MyClass program and a system folder, keep them together) ➔ close MyClass ➔ copy the whole bin folder from the old folder into the new one (next to the program) ➔ start the new version. The database is backed up automatically on first start; delete the old folder once your classes are confirmed. Installed edition: just run the new installer; your data is kept.
• Not now: click Skip this version and it is not shown again until a newer one appears, or press ✕ to hide it for this session.
• To check or turn it off yourself: the “🔄 Software Update” card under ⚙️ Settings ➔ 🔐 Password & Connection shows the current version, has “Check for updates now”, and a checkbox for automatic checks.
Only the latest version number is requested from GitHub; no class, student or personal data is sent, at most once every 12 hours. If the school network blocks outside connections the check fails silently and nothing else is affected.
升級備份BACKUP
升級與還原資料Upgrading and restoring data
• 升級：直接執行新版安裝程式即可。只會換掉程式檔，bin 裡的資料不會被動到。
• 自動備份：程式更新後第一次啟動，系統會先把資料庫備份到 bin\backups（保留最近 10 份）。
• 還原：先把程式結束，把備份檔複製成 bin\classroom_record.db（並刪除同資料夾的 -wal、-shm 檔），再重新啟動。
• 學生上傳的檔案在 bin\uploads，安裝版也可以從通知區圖示選「開啟檔案資料夾」。
• 搬家：從可攜版換成安裝版時，安裝程式會問你要不要匯入原本的資料夾（含 bin 的那一層），原資料夾不會被改動。
• Upgrade: just run the new installer. It only replaces program files and never touches the bin data.
• Automatic backup: on the first start after an update the database is copied to bin\backups (last 10 kept).
• Restore: quit the program, copy the backup to bin\classroom_record.db (and delete the -wal and -shm files beside it), then start again.
• Student uploads are in bin\uploads; the installed edition also offers “Open files folder” in the tray icon.
• Moving: when switching from portable to installed, the installer offers to import your old folder (the one containing bin); the original is left untouched.

問Q
學生手機掃了 QR Code 卻開不起來？Students scan the QR Code but the page won’t open?
請看前面「讓學生與手機連線」的最後一張卡片：確認同一個 Wi-Fi、iPhone 先關閉行動數據、出現警告時點「顯示詳細資訊 ➔ 造訪此網站」。

問Q
不小心給錯分數了？Gave the wrong points?
剛加完，按下方出現的「↩️ 復原」。已經過了一段時間，到該生的評分歷程刪除那一筆；刪錯了可按「↩️ 恢復紀錄」。
Right after awarding, press “↩️ Undo” at the bottom. Later, delete the entry in the student’s history; if deleted by mistake press “↩️ Restore”.
問Q
學生忘記帳號或密碼？A student forgot the account or password?
先請學生在登入畫面多輸入幾次，錯 3 次後會顯示他自己寫的「密碼提醒」。還是想不起來，到 ⚙️ 設定 ➔ 👨‍🎓 學生名冊管理，按該生的 🔑 恢復預設密碼（回到學號），學生下次登入再重新設定。
Ask the student to try a few times on the login page; after 3 wrong attempts the hint they wrote is shown. If it does not help, go to ⚙️ Settings ➔ 👨‍🎓 Student Roster and press 🔑 Restore default password (back to the student ID); the student sets a new one at next login.
問Q
學生說相機打不開、照片很模糊？Camera won’t open or photos look blurry?
請學生第一次按「允許」使用相機與麥克風，並用「🎥 高畫質拍攝」。如果還是不行，改用手機的相機 App 拍好，再選檔案上傳。
Ask the student to tap “Allow” for camera and microphone the first time, and use “🎥 High-quality capture”. If it still fails, shoot with the phone’s camera app and upload the file.
問Q
期末想一次結算成績？How do I summarise at the end of term?
到 🏆 激勵與統計 ➔ 📊 報表資料匯出，選整學期的日期範圍，下載 Excel。
Go to 🏆 Rewards & Reports ➔ 📊 Reports, choose the whole term’s dates and download the Excel file.
重要IMPORTANT
資料會不會外流？Will my data leak?
會，只要「程式連同 bin 資料夾」落到別人手上。
班級、學生、成績、照片與備份都放在程式旁的 bin 資料夾，沒有加密；對方可以在自己的電腦開啟，並用「忘記密碼」重設後讀取全部資料，登入密碼擋不住這種情況。
• 隨身碟、筆電請妥善保管，離開座位時鎖定螢幕，公用電腦用完請刪除 bin。
• 分享程式給別人時，只給執行檔與 system 資料夾，不要附上 bin；也不要把整個資料夾寄信或上傳到雲端。
• bin\backups 的備份也含有全部資料。
• 隨身碟遺失或電腦被盜，請視同學生個資外洩，依學校規定通報。
• 只有「程式」外流（沒有 bin）不會洩漏任何資料。
• 需要更高保護時，請用 Windows BitLocker 或加密隨身碟保護整顆磁碟。
登入畫面與首頁都有這個提醒，設定 ➔ 密碼與連線 也有完整說明。同一個區網的其他人無法使用「忘記密碼」；多次輸入錯誤密碼時，系統會暫時拒絕那個裝置的登入。
Yes, if “the program together with the bin folder” falls into someone else’s hands.
Classes, students, scores, photos and backups live in the bin folder next to the program, unencrypted; anyone can open it on their own computer, reset the password with “Forgot password” and read everything. The login password cannot stop this.
• Keep USB drives and laptops safe, lock the screen when away, and delete bin from shared computers after use.
• When sharing the program give only the executable and the system folder, never bin; do not email or upload the whole folder.
• Backups in bin\backups contain everything too.
• If a USB drive is lost or a computer is stolen, treat it as a student data breach and report it per school rules.
• A leak of only the program (without bin) reveals no data.
• For stronger protection use Windows BitLocker or an encrypted USB drive for the whole disk.
This reminder is shown on the login page and the home page, with full details under Settings ➔ Password & Connection. Others on the same network cannot use “Forgot password”; after repeated wrong passwords the system temporarily refuses login from that device.
問Q
資料會不會不見？Will my data be lost?
資料都存在你這台電腦（或隨身碟）的 bin 資料夾裡，不會上傳到網路。升級前系統會自動備份。建議學期結束時，把整個 bin 資料夾複製一份留存。
All data stays in the bin folder on your computer (or USB drive) and is never uploaded. The system backs up before upgrades. At the end of term, copy the whole bin folder somewhere safe.
